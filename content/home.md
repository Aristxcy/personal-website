@POMS China, Hangzhou, 2023

My name is Chenyu Xue (薛晨予). I am now an Assistant Professor/Lecturer at School of Business, East China University of Science and Technology. I obtained my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering (MS&E) from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE) in 2025, where I was fortunately supervised by Professor [Bo Jiang](https://sites.google.com/site/isyebojiang/). I received my bachelor's degree in Engineering from Chongqing University (CQU) in 2020.

My research interests intersect optimization, reinforcement learning, statistics, and mechanism design, with a focus on assortment and pricing optimization problems in revenue management, online resource allocation, and supply chain management.

I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at Department of Industrial Systems Engineering & Management (ISEM), National University of Singapore (NUS), and supervised by Professor [Xiaobo Li](https://sites.google.com/site/lixiaobohome/home).
